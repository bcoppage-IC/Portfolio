SNHU · ECO 327 Economic Development · 2026

A Binding Constraint: Corruption and Economic Development in Brazil

Brazil's corruption is a path-dependent institutional equilibrium, not a series of isolated scandals. Reform efforts are reabsorbed because elites capture the institutions that would otherwise hold them accountable.

  • Analyzed Brazil's corruption through the lens of institutional economics.
  • Drew on Todaro and Smith's path dependency framework, peer-reviewed studies of the Petrobras cartel and Lava Jato, and current Transparency International data.
  • Built a verification workflow that checked every factual claim against its source document before drafting.

The project taught me to distinguish evidence from inference, to trace claims back to their exact source sentences, and to verify derived statistics rather than accepting them as reported findings. Those are transferable skills for any research or analytical role. Read the paper
